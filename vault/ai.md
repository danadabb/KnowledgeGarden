### Inference

- The process that a trained machine learning model uses to draw conclusions from brand-new data e.g. a self driving car on a road it's never driven on before recognising a stop sign

**AI Inference vs. training**
Training is the first phase for an AI model involving a process of trial and error. 
Inference is the process that follows AI training.

**Use cases**:
- LLMs - trained on sample text and can parse and interpet texts it's not seen before
